Ball In The Box

CTE Engineering Pathway: Pacific Grove High School

My Role

I did the CAD and CAM in Fusion 360 and machined this on a Tormach 1100MX CNC mill.

I used an endmill for roughing and a ball-end mill for the spherical faces. The feeds were too aggressive for the aluminum, so I switched to plywood.

What happened

  • Splintering on some faces from the wood grain
  • I'd try a different vise setup to hold the part more securely
