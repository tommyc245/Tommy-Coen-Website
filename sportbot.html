SportBot

Superhero-Themed Rube Goldberg Machine — Duke University

My Role

4-person team, part of a larger integrated performance group. Designed the elevator mechanically and CAD'd the track for both boxes my team built. Helped with electronics. One of the leads integrating all the boxes together on the final night.

Officially "Design Challenge 4: Play Ball!" — SportBot for short. Each team of 4 builds a box that receives a ball, runs it through two sensor-triggered actions (one Arduino-controlled, one pure analog circuit), then passes the ball to the next team's box. Teams chain their boxes together into one integrated performance group, ending in a finale action. Each box had to fit roughly within a 4 ft height and a 3x3 ft footprint.

Our group ran a superhero theme. My team built the Spider-Man and Wonder Woman boxes, connected by an elevator that carries the ball between them.

Results

  • Two successful full-group runs within the allotted time — 100%
