SportBot

Superhero-Themed Rube Goldberg Machine: Duke University

My Role

I designed the elevator and CAD'd the track for both boxes on our 4-person team. I helped with electronics and was one of the leads connecting the group's boxes on the final night.

Our team built the Spider-Man and Wonder Woman boxes for SportBot, a superhero-themed Rube Goldberg machine.

The setup

  • EGR 121 project, officially Design Challenge 4: Play Ball!
  • Teams of 4, each passing a ball through a box to the next team, ending in a finale action
  • Two sensor-triggered actions per box: one Arduino-controlled and one analog circuit
  • Each box roughly within a 4 ft height and a 3x3 ft footprint
  • Our two boxes connected by an elevator carrying the ball between them

Team result

  • Two successful full-group runs within the allotted time: 100%
