Seed Sheller

Roller-Based Sunflower Seed Sheller — Duke University

Whiteboard results photo — add to images/DukeCoursework/
My Role

4-person team. Designed and CAD'd the rollers, drive mechanism, and funnel.

A roller-based mechanism that shells sunflower seeds while keeping the kernel intact. The key insight, found through iteration, was in the funnel geometry: it has to orient each seed so it enters the rollers along its seam, so the shell fractures along the seam instead of crushing the kernel off-seam.

Results

  • 41 intact kernels in a 3-minute run — top score of 8 teams on a yield-minus-waste metric
