Filament Winder

Machine Tuning & Composite Tube Development

My Role

Structures Lead (2026–present). Tuning and troubleshooting the filament winder for future fiberglass and carbon flight tube production.

This is the team's first year attempting filament winding; body tubes were previously laid up by hand. I am pushing for the switch to make production faster and cheaper, with potentially stronger tubes. Those benefits have not been measured.

I ran a complete test wind in October 2026. Machine tuning and troubleshooting are ongoing. We have not produced a finished filament-wound tube yet. Stress-strain characterization is still pending.

Failure & next iteration

The reused 3D printer stepper motor was not powerful enough and skipped during the test wind. The 3D printed gear system was also not strong enough.

The team is replacing the motor and gear system. The replacement system has not yet been validated, and no finished filament-wound tubes have been produced.
