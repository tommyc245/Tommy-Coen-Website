Filament Winder

Future Fiberglass & Carbon Flight Tubes

My Role

As Structures Lead, I'm bringing online the team's first ever filament winder for future fiberglass and carbon flight tubes.

I ran a complete test wind in October 2026. The team is replacing the motor and gear system as testing continues. We're aiming for faster, cheaper, potentially stronger tubes than hand layup.

  • No finished filament-wound tubes yet
  • Stress-strain characterization still pending

Winding trials

  • Carriage moving along the rotating aluminum mandrel to lay down fiber

Prepreg practice

The team made a prepreg tube to practice layup as a fallback while working on the winder.
