About

I'm a sophomore studying Mechanical Engineering at Duke University, currently Structures Lead for Duke Aero's two-stage rocket program. I grew up in Pacific Grove, California, and spent four years on FRC Team 5104 (the BreakerBots), working my way from machining parts on the shop floor to Co-Captain and CAD Lead.

Over four seasons I took on progressively more responsibility: machinist on Charybdis (2022), Manufacturing Lead and Drive Coach for Kraken and Cthulhu (2023), Manufacturing Lead again for Plop that same year's off-season, Co-Captain and Manufacturing Lead for Typhoon (2024), and finally Co-Captain with full CAD and manufacturing ownership for Leviathan (2025).

I completed the CTE Engineering Pathway in high school, going through the full Titans of CNC machining program and building hands-on experience with manual mills, lathes, and CNC equipment from scratch.

At Duke I joined Duke Aero as a freshman, working across solid propulsion and structures on Devil's Advocate, which placed 3rd in the 30,000 ft SRAD category at IREC 2026. I'm now Structures Lead for the team's next vehicle, a two-stage rocket targeting IREC 2027. I'm interested in problems that sit at the intersection of mechanical design, manufacturing, and controls, especially in robotics and aerospace.
