Plop

FIRST Robotics Competition: Charged Up Off-Season

My Role

I led manufacturing and handled CAD lead work. I designed the chassis, adjusted the team's Grey-T elevator, and CAM'd and machined a large share of the parts.

We used Plop to try new systems and competed at Madtown Throwdown.

The work

  • Elevator stages kept inside the frame perimeter when retracted, with enough reach for all three grid levels
  • Manufacturing subteam grew to 15+ machinists
  • Team robot: SDS swerve drive and an elevator/wrist system for cones and cubes, with autonomous scoring in all grid levels, including during teleop
