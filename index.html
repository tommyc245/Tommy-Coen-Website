About

I'm a sophomore studying Mechanical Engineering at Duke University, pursuing an Aerospace Engineering Certificate alongside my degree. I'm currently Structures Lead for Duke Aero's next vehicles — a 30k SRAD rocket and a bare-bones two-stage staging tester rocket, both targeting IREC 2027 — building on Devil's Advocate, which placed 3rd in the 30,000 ft SRAD category at IREC 2026.

I grew up in Pacific Grove, California, and spent four years on FRC Team 5104 (the BreakerBots), progressing from machinist to Co-Captain and CAD Lead across Charybdis, Kraken/Cthulhu, Typhoon, and Leviathan. I also completed the CTE Engineering Pathway in high school, going through the Titans of CNC program and building hands-on experience with manual machining and CNC equipment from scratch.
